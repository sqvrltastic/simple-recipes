Balkan fish stew — Riblja Čorba

Ingredients

  • 3 onions
  • 3 small carrots
  • 1 parsley root
  • 3 small pointy red peppers (mild)
  • 2 tomatoes
  • half of a bulb of garlic
  • 1 fresh parsley bunch
  • 500 ml tomato sauce (without added sugar)
  • 1-2 tbsp salted veggie spice mix (without added sugar or artificial flavor enhancers)
  • hot water
  • cheap (NOT extra virgin) olive oil for frying
  • 800 g to 1kg any fish or a mix of fishes (I will use local Oslić fillet a.k.a. Hake a.k.a Merluccius Merluccius)
  • 4-5 dried laurel/bay leaves
  • 1tsp crushed white pepper
  • 1tsp crushed black pepper
  • 250 ml white whine

For thickening agent:

  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 50 ml cool water

Directions

Submerge tomatoes in boiled water for a brief period, then remove their skin.

Chop onions, add salt and fry them on olive oil in a pot until they caramelize, then take them out and put them in a separate bowl for fried veggies.

Chop carrots and parsley root, add a bit of salt and fry them until they soften, then take them out and put them together with fried onions.

Chop tomatoes and red peppers, add a bit of salt and fry them until they soften, then add chopped garlic, fry it a bit until garlic becomes a bit orange and then add all other fried veggies back together. Pour some hot water until all veggies are covered, add a spoonful of veggie spice mix, cover and let it simmer for 10 minutes on medium heat.

Add more water until you get a soupy consistency. Do a taste test, add veggie seasoning mix and white and black pepper. Add toss in some big fish chunks, I just eyeball it, but let's say around 800 g. I just add as many fish fillet chunks as I can fit in the cooking pot. 😝 Toss in 4-5 dried laurel leaves at the top. Cover and cook for 30 mins on medium heat. When the time is up, remove from heat temporarily.

In the meantime, chop fresh parsley and prepare a thickening agent by mixing flout and water in a bowl, until you get a smooth liquid without any lumps.

Take fish chunks out, place them in a separate bowl and let them cool a little bit. Break them with your fingers (careful! they can still be hot, use a fork if you need).

Add parsley and flout and water mix into the soup. Add more seasonings if you want and stir well. Add white whine. Toss the smaller fish chunks back in and remove from heat. Remove any bay leaf that you see.

The stew is ready to be served!
